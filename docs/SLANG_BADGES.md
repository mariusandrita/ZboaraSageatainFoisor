# Darts Slang — Celebrations & Badges for Garage Play

> Curated for amateurs throwing wobbly darts in a garage. No professionals were harmed.
> Use these as badge names, celebration callouts, or just to heckle your mates.

---

## Hall of Fame — Great Shots (Positive Badges)

| Badge Name | Trigger | Description |
|------------|---------|-------------|
| **Robin Hood** | Dart lands inside another dart's shaft/flight | You didn't score. You did art. Legendary and completely useless. |
| **Nine-Darter** | 501 finished in exactly 9 darts | The holy grail. If this happens in the garage, stop the music. |
| **Maximum / Ton 80** | Score 180 (three treble 20s) | Three arrows, one number, zero excuses for missing after this. |
| **Big Fish** | Checkout on 170 (T20 + T20 + Bull) | The highest possible finish. Tell your grandchildren. |
| **Little Fish** | Checkout on 160 (T20 + S20 + Bull) | Still a big fish in a garage pond. |
| **Shanghai** | Single + double + triple of the same number in one turn | Sorcery. Illegal in some garages. |
| **Three in a Bed** | All three darts in the same segment | Snug. Very snug. The darts are basically cuddling. |
| **Champagne Breakfast** | T20 + T5 + T1 in one turn | You aimed for 180 and still made something beautiful. |
| **Champagne Finish** | Win a leg with a double bullseye | Pop the fizzy water. It's all you've got in the garage. |
| **Black Eye / Top Hat** | All three darts in the bullseye in one round | The board is scared of you now. |
| **Tops** | Finish on double 20 | The classic. The crowd goes mild. |

## Hall of Shame — Disaster Badges

| Badge Name | Trigger | Description |
|------------|---------|-------------|
| **Bag of Nails** | Three single 1s in one round | The worst three darts legally possible. You threw three darts and scored 3. |
| **Madhouse** | Left on double 1 to win | You are one dart from glory. It is a tiny target. You are in the madhouse now. Good luck. |
| **Bust** | Scored over your remaining total | You were so close. Then you weren't. Score resets, pride does not. |
| **Wet Feet** | Stepped over the oche while throwing | You fouled. The dart doesn't count. The shame does. |
| **Right Church, Wrong Pew** | Hit the right ring but wrong number | Technically impressive. Strategically catastrophic. Also known as "Daddy's Bed." |
| **Circle It** | Score under 10 in a turn | The crowd draws a circle around the score. Sometimes a fish. Sometimes a portrait. |
| **Bounce-Out** | Dart bounces off the board entirely | The board has rejected you. Personally. |
| **Champagne Shower** | Bust on a finishing dart | You threw for the win. You overshot. Someone is getting wet. |

| **Mugs Away** | Loses the previous leg, throws first next | You lost. Now you go first. Rub it in gently. |
| **Dead Eye** | Hits the exact center of the bull | The pixel. The void. Perfection in a tiny red dot. |
| **Lipstick** | Hits treble 20 | Kisses the most important segment on the board. |

## Bonus: Things Shouted in Garage Darts Culture

- **"Game on!"** — It begins.
- **"Game shot!"** — It ends.
- **"Arrers!"** — British pub shorthand for darts. Say it with an accent.
- **"Mugs away!"** — Loser goes first. Tradition.
- **"Circle it!"** — When someone scores embarrassingly low. Draw the fish.
- **"Wet feet!"** — Someone crossed the line. Disallow the dart, enjoy the chaos.
- **"Robin Hood!"** — Everyone stops and stares at the board in disbelief.

---

> Most of these originated in British pub and working-class darts culture.
> They have been adapted here for a garage setting where the beer is cold,
> the oche is a strip of tape on the floor, and nobody really knows the rules.
